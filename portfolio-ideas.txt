Portfolio ideas for later — Giovanni Cascio / 高景浩

1. A “Now” section
   Briefly share what you’re learning, building, or photographing this semester.
   Keep it short and easy to update.

2. Photo essays
   Create small collections from Taiwan or France, with captions explaining
   what caught your attention. Start with a Taiwan photo essay.
   Gather original image files for a larger, photo-only gallery with optional
   captions and links to the original Instagram posts.

3. First journal post
   Write about your Mandarin immersion experience at Providence University.
   Connect language learning, travel, and photography using your own stories.

4. Robotics case study
   Once materials are ready, add real robot photos, a demo video, your role,
   and one technical problem you solved. The AprilTag localization work could
   be a useful focus.

5. Internship availability
   If you’re seeking internships, add a short line specifying the roles and
   dates you’re targeting. Confirm those details before publishing.

Suggested next addition: a Taiwan photo essay.
These are future ideas, not changes scheduled for implementation.
